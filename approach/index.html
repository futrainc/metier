Implementation approach

From discovery to steady state.

A five-stage journey that de-risks change, aligns stakeholders early and builds ownership at every step — so the results last after we leave.

  1. 01

    Discover

    0–2 weeks

    • Map expectations
    • Data & benchmark scan
    • Interview stakeholders
    • Opportunity sizing
    • Evaluate redundancies
    • Confirm the sponsor
  2. 02

    Design

    3–6 weeks

    • Envision the target state
    • Insighting & hypothesis testing
    • De-risk the roadmap
    • Clarify key priorities
    • Assess structures & linkages
    • Leadership behaviors
  3. 03

    Define

    7–10 weeks

    • Create a business case
    • Align stakeholders
    • Evaluate capability v/s capacity
    • Assess enterprise readiness
    • Prepare pilot
    • Change adoption sign-off
  4. 04

    Deploy

    11–30+ weeks

    • Equip key stakeholders
    • Execute pilot
    • Create project management office
    • Monitor ownership
    • Capture redundancies
    • Celebrate small wins
  5. 05

    Derive

    Ongoing

    • Achieve steady state
    • Fortify progress achieved
    • Industrialize & automate
    • Solve scalability constraints
    • KPI governance & cadence
    • Continuous improvement

The highlighted stage marks the highest-effort, highest-duration phase of the journey.
